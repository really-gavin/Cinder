Cinder Wallet.

Your Cinder card on your phone. At a shop, hold your phone near theirs and Cinder Pay pops up: tap Pay, touch the fingerprint sensor, done. It also pays online shops with one button, scans checkout codes, and takes payments if you sell things.

Download Cinder Wallet (2 MB)

Mine or run a node.

A spare Android phone can mine Cinder or run a node with the Cinder Phone app. It mines only while plugged in and pauses when it gets warm.

Just install the app

Keeps everything on the phone. Install it, open it, pick Mine Cinder and type your card ID.

Full setup on a spare phone
