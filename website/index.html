Jobs

Post a job and its pay is locked on the network until the work is done. Approve it, or let the review day run out, and the worker is paid. Nobody sits in the middle holding the money.

See open jobs

Loans

Ask for CDR to get something going. Anyone can fund it at the terms you set. If it doesn't work out, either side can close it and whatever's left goes straight back.

See loan requests

Pay

Every wallet is a card with a 16-character ID, read out in fours like a card number. Payments arrive in about a minute and cost nothing: your device does a second of work instead of paying a fee.

Get your card

It cools, it doesn't halve.

Bitcoin cuts its reward in half overnight every four years. Cinder lets it slide instead: within each year the reward eases down a little every block, landing at exactly half by the end. Miners never wake up to a cliff.

The total that will ever exist is fixed at 31,536,000 CDR, one for every second in a year.

The rules.

These are enforced by every node. A block that breaks one is thrown away, no matter how much work went into it.

Run a node and you enforce them too.

Get on the network.

You need Python 3.9 or newer. That's it. These five steps take about two minutes.

  1. Get your card

    Make a wallet on the wallet page. You choose a 6-digit PIN, and you get a card ID, a payment code (SPP) and 12 backup words. That's all you need to send, receive, take jobs and borrow.

    On Android, get the Cinder Wallet app: pay at shops by holding your phone near theirs, and pay online with one button.

  2. Earn your first CDR

    Everyone starts at zero. Take a job from the job board, ask someone to pay you, or mine blocks yourself with the steps below.

  3. Download Cinder to mine

    You need Python 3.9 or newer. Unzip the Cinder folder, open a terminal in it and install the one library it needs.

    pip install ecdsa

  4. Start a node

    Your node keeps a full copy of the chain and checks every block against the rules. Point it at someone already on the network.

    python node.py --peer http://SEED-NODE:8642

    The node serves this website too, at http://localhost:8642.

  5. Mine to your card

    The miner uses every CPU core. Each block you find pays your card the current reward plus any fees in it.

    python miner.py --id "YOUR CARD ID0"

Mine with an Android phone

Any Android phone (Android 8 or newer) can mine Cinder. It mines while it's plugged in and pauses when it gets warm. There are two ways to set it up.

Just the app. Scan the first code with the phone's camera, install Cinder, tap Open in Cinder, and type your card ID.

Full setup on a spare phone. Factory reset it (this erases everything), tap the same spot on the welcome screen 6 times, connect to Wi-Fi, and scan the second code. The phone becomes a dedicated miner. "Release this phone" in the app hands it back.

This is testnet. Coins here are for building and testing, and the chain may be reset before a public launch.
